# Campaign Mode — Preserved Concept

**Status:** Removed from CFO Fitness. Kept here as a design record for a separate app.

Campaign Mode was a fantasy-RPG progression layer over the same daily score the
CFO produces. It was feature-flagged (`campaignModeEnabled`, default `false`) and
never shipped as a default experience. It is being pulled out because it is a
different product wearing the same codebase — not because the idea failed.

> **Full implementation:** commit `e166e174a136666bbf7fef8c8d915185886c64a3`
> (the commit immediately before removal). Everything below exists there in
> working form — 22 levels, the engine, the Genkit brief flow, TTS narration.
> `git show e166e174:src/lib/campaign/roadmap.ts` and siblings.

## The core idea

The CFO scores your day in points. Campaign Mode spent those points as progress
through a 20-level narrative rather than a financial rank ladder. Same input,
entirely different emotional frame: the portfolio metaphor rewards *solvency*,
the campaign metaphor rewards *holding the line*.

The two framings do not mix well in one app — which is the argument for making
this its own thing.

## What made it good

**The premise reframes failure as neglect, not defeat.** From `lore.ts`:

> "The realm was not lost to an invading army. It was lost slowly — to neglect,
> to years of the watch going unkept. No single day undid the realm. It was a
> thousand small ones, compounding."

That is the actual thesis of habit tracking, stated better than a progress bar
states it. The inverse — a thousand small good days compounding — is the product.

**The protagonist is deliberately unchosen.**

> "You are not a chosen hero handed a sword. You are a watcher who decided, one
> ordinary morning, to take up a post nobody assigned you. That is the whole of
> the origin story, and it is enough."

**The reign has no final chapter.** Reaching Legend Status ends the climb but not
the story — a secured realm still needs tending, with stability that rises or
slips based on upkeep. This solves the standard fitness-gamification failure of
having nothing after the goal weight.

## Mechanical design

**Three tiers across 20 levels** — escalating scope of responsibility:

| Tier | Levels | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Local Hero | 1–5 | the immediate borders |
| Regional Commander | 6–15 | expeditions beyond them |
| Realm Sovereign | 16–20 | the whole realm |

**Dual gating — points *and* elapsed days.** Each level required
`points_to_advance` (600–1200+) *and* a `min_days` floor (7–14). You could not
sprint a level by having three excellent days; the realm takes weeks by design.
This is the mechanic most worth carrying forward — it prevents the burst-and-quit
cycle that plain point thresholds encourage.

**Legendary Relics — body-composition gates.** Two hard gates blocked advancement
past Levels 8 and 13, keyed to real measurements rather than points:

- *The Sub-205 Sigil* — `weightLbs <= 205`
- *The Fatless Crown* — `bodyFatPct <= 26`

Points measure effort; Relics measure outcome. Requiring both is what kept the
narrative honest — you could not narrate your way to a physique.

> Note: these thresholds were hardcoded to the original author's targets. Any
> revival needs them derived per-user.

**Item grants per level** — `item_tier` 1–3, one grant per level up
(`item_travelers_ration`, `item_potion_of_rest`, `item_whetstone`,
`item_wardens_ledger`…). Flavor rewards, no mechanical effect. See `items.ts`.

**Hand-authored lore, LLM-generated dailies.** Static world backstory in
`lore.ts` so the premise never drifts, with a per-day Brief generated by a Genkit
flow (`campaign-brief.ts`) against `buildBriefContext()`. The split is the right
one — the LLM colors inside lines it cannot redraw.

**Replay-based state.** `replayHistoryToSheet()` rebuilt the character sheet from
score history rather than storing mutable progress, so the sheet was always
derivable and never drifted from the underlying ledger. Same discipline as
`score-backfill.ts`. Worth keeping in any rebuild.

**Catch-up handling.** `getCatchupDates()` reconciled missed days rather than
silently skipping them — you returned to a realm that had noticed your absence.

## What to reconsider if rebuilt

- **Relic thresholds must be per-user.** Hardcoded 205 lb / 26% is meaningless to
  anyone else and would read as broken.
- **TTS narration** (`campaign-tts.ts`, `tts.ts`) carried a real cost per
  generated brief via `@google-cloud/text-to-speech`. Verify it earns its keep
  before rebuilding it.
- **The 20-level arc is long** — roughly 6–9 months at the specified `min_days`
  floors. Right for the theme, but validate that anyone reaches Level 6 before
  building levels 7–20.
- **Two metaphors, one app was the actual problem.** Financial and fantasy
  framings each work; interleaved they diluted both. If rebuilt, commit fully.
